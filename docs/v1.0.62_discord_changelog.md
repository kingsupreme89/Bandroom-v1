🏈 **Bandroom v1.0.62 is out!** (covers v1.0.61 too — back-to-back releases same day)

Big one — a full pass on the "make it feel like Spotify/iTunes" redesign, plus a bunch of real bugs found and fixed along the way.

**🎧 Auto-Assign**
- New **Auto-Assign** pill on the event dashboard — fills any empty song slots for whichever team you're editing, one click, any time. It only ever fills gaps, never touches songs you've already picked yourself.

**🎨 CoverFlow redesign**
- The team picker and Favorite Team screen are now a big iTunes/Front Row-style CoverFlow — giant reflective album covers instead of the old compact carousel.
- "Choose a Team" (the full team browser) is now that same CoverFlow instead of a plain grid — browse with the arrows, click the center cover to pick it.
- The background now glows in your active team's color and shifts live as you browse teams in any picker — no more getting stuck on whatever team was active before you opened the screen.
- School names in Sound Bank and the marketplace now glow each team's own color.

**📁 Sound Bank cleanup**
- The Default Song Pack list in a team's Sound Bank used to be one long alphabetical wall of songs ("Defense_Drive Starter_4", "_5", "_6"...) — it's now grouped into collapsible folders by situation type (Kickoff, Drive Starter, etc.).

**📥 Import feedback**
- Song pack imports used to finish before you could even see a progress bar move. Now shows real per-file progress with filenames scrolling by as it works, and a proper "here's what got imported and where to find it" confirmation that stays on screen until you dismiss it — with an option to turn that off and just get a quiet toast instead.

**🛠️ Bug fixes**
- The ✕ button on your Profile page didn't do anything — fixed.
- Escape key didn't close the Favorite Team picker — fixed.
- Fixed a couple of edge cases (found in this release's own internal audit) where a failed save could leave the background glow or the first-run team picker in a stuck/inconsistent state.
- Fixed the side panel not shrinking properly on smaller windows.

Update should grab automatically next time you open the app. If not, grab it here: https://github.com/kingsupreme89/Bandroom-v1/releases/tag/v1.0.62
