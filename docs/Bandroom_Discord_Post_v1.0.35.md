**New:**
* 🖼️ **61 new team logos** — Air Force, Akron, Appalachian State, Arkansas State, Ball State, Boise State, Bowling Green, Buffalo, Central Michigan, Charlotte, Coastal Carolina, Colorado State, East Carolina, Eastern Michigan, FAU, FIU, Fresno State, Georgia Southern, Georgia State, Hawaii, Jacksonville State, James Madison, Kennesaw State, Kent State, Liberty, Louisiana, Louisiana Tech, Marshall, Maryland, Memphis, Miami OH, Middle Tennessee, Navy, Nevada, New Mexico, New Mexico State, North Texas, Ohio, Old Dominion, Rice, Sam Houston, San Diego State, San Jose State, SMU, South Alabama, South Florida, Southern Miss, Temple, Texas State, Toledo, Troy, Tulane, Tulsa, UAB, UNLV, UTEP, UTSA, Utah State, Western Kentucky, Western Michigan, Wyoming.
* ✂️ **Set your own team logo** — in the Teams panel, hover any team tile and hit the pencil icon. Upload any image, drag to reposition, use the slider to zoom, and whatever's inside the square becomes that team's logo everywhere in the app. No more waiting on us to source a logo for your school.
* 🎨 **Team colors pulse everywhere now** — button presses, panel flashes, and other glowing accents follow your active team's color across the whole app instead of a fixed cyan. The one place that stays neutral on purpose: the matchup screen, since you're picking *both* teams there.
* ⚡ **VS split-screen fixed** — the glowing line dividing the two teams on the matchup backdrop was sitting slightly off from where the two team backgrounds actually meet, and its pulse was barely visible (opacity-only). Now it's properly centered and pulses with real brightness/glow, so both teams' colors are easy to see dividing the screen.
