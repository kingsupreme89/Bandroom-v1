🏈 **Bandroom v1.1.20 + v1.1.21 are out — HBCU Mode's scoring sequence finally plays by real football rules**

Two sessions rolled into one changelog since .19. Most of the work is HBCU Mode's touchdown/kickoff/pot sequencing — it's been rebuilt from the ground up to actually match how a real game flows.

**🏟️ HBCU Mode: the pot now stops and starts the way it should**
- On a score (touchdown OR field goal), the pot goes silent, the Kickoff cue plays, and the pot only picks back up **20 seconds after that kickoff song actually finishes** — no more cutting back in early, no more random "bonus song" squeezed in between that nobody asked for.
- **Field goals were being ignored entirely** — the pot didn't stop, and the away team's kickoff routing broke (it'd fall back and play Home's kickoff instead). Both fixed.
- **Two pot songs can't play over each other anymore.** Home and Away are now hard-guaranteed to never overlap, even if something else races the schedule.
- **Pregame Tunnel** is now a real assignable event card in HBCU Mode, alongside a fix for Touchdown (and others) sometimes going missing from the situations list depending on which tab was open when you switched into HBCU Mode.

**🎛️ Pot dashboard: Pause actually pauses now**
Pressing Pause used to just freeze the *scheduler* — whatever was already playing kept right on playing, and hitting Resume would start a second song on top of it. Pause now actually silences the current song. Also added a **Skip** button to jump straight to the next side's turn instead of waiting it out.

**🎚️ Volume sliders update live now**
Dragging the volume slider on a Team Pot song or an event card used to have zero effect on whatever was already previewing — you'd have to close and reopen the preview to hear the change. Now it updates in real time while you drag, same as the Master/Home/Away sliders already did.

**🔊 Windows Volume Mixer can't silently mute the app anymore**
Bandroom now checks its own slider in Windows' Volume Mixer once a second and snaps it back to 100%/unmuted if anything pulls it down — a fresh audio session (like a new preview click) doesn't always inherit the last one's volume, and this was quietly dragging Bandroom's volume down without anyone touching it.

**🥁 Punchier Drums, actually punchier**
Retuned the effect — stronger attack boost on the hit, plus a high-end trim so the extra energy reads as *punch*, not harshness/cymbal wash.

**📥 Import Your Own Song now matches every other clipper**
The naming step and trim step both moved into the same in-app UI every other trim flow already uses (only the actual file picker is still a native Windows dialog, since that's picking a file off your own disk). Also fixed a layering bug where that naming popup — and the trim panel right after it — could render invisible behind the My Downloads window.
