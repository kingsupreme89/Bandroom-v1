🏈 **Bandroom — What's New (v1.0.31 → v1.0.40)**

Big stretch of updates. Here's everything that changed, in plain English:

**🎵 The Marketplace got a full community upgrade**
You can now upload your own songs and team backgrounds for anyone to grab — and see everyone else's. Like your favorites, report anything that shouldn't be there, check out the leaderboard for which schools are getting the most love, and search by team. Downloaded something? It goes straight into **My Downloads**, ready to use immediately.

**📥 My Downloads**
Every song and image you've pulled from the Marketplace lives in one clean library now, clearly labeled by school and name. No more digging around trying to remember what you grabbed.

**🖼️ 61 new team logos, plus your own logo tool**
We sourced and added 61 real team logos across every conference. And if a team's still missing, you don't have to wait on us anymore — there's a built-in crop tool. Upload any image, drag it into place, zoom with a slider, and save. Instant, clean, perfectly square every time.

**🎨 Team colors are alive everywhere**
Buttons, panels, glows — everything in the app now pulses and lights up in YOUR team's colors. It's a completely different feel than a flat gray UI.

**🏆 GAMETIME just got louder**
Hitting GAMETIME now plays a real football tackle hit instead of a generic chime — it feels like kickoff.

**👤 Sign in with Google — now actually works**
This was scaffolded for a while and it's finally live. Sign in and Bandroom remembers you.

**⭐ Your Favorite Team, saved for good**
Pick a favorite team in your Profile and the app instantly switches to their colors — and remembers it every time you relaunch. Once you're signed in, that favorite (plus your stats) follows you across every device you use Bandroom on.

**📊 Your stats, tracked automatically**
Games watched, songs triggered, uploads, downloads — Bandroom now keeps score on your own usage, right in your Profile.

**🛠️ Behind-the-scenes reliability fixes**
A handful of real bugs got squashed this stretch: a rare issue where two downloads happening at once could quietly lose one of them, a spot where "My Downloads" wasn't rendering right, and a marketplace hiccup that could make uploads/browsing act up under heavy traffic — all fixed and confirmed working.

**✨ Polish pass**
Fonts across every button and menu now match cleanly. The Bandroom "B" logo got a slick glowing outline look. The VS matchup screen's divider line is now perfectly centered with a real glow instead of a faint flicker.

---

That's the recap. Bandroom's come a long way — thanks for testing, reporting bugs, and sending logos. Keep it coming. 🎺
