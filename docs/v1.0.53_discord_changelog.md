🏈 **Bandroom v1.0.53 is out!**

Big one under the hood — fixed a bunch of audio bugs, a couple of crashes, and locked down some security stuff. Here's what's different:

**🔊 Audio fixes**
- Missed field goals now actually make a sound! Before, if the kick missed, Bandroom just stayed silent — now your defense gets its own cue for it.
- Fixed a rare case where two different songs could try to play at the exact same moment and glitch/cut each other off.
- Fixed a case where a tackle-for-loss could double-fire its sound.

**🛠️ Crash fixes**
- Fixed "Duplicate Profile To..." (right-click a team) crashing the app.
- Fixed the Mac version not being able to build at all.

**🎵 Marketplace / Sound Bank**
- Fixed a bug where a team's Sound Bank or Trophy Room could show *zero* songs/images even though real uploads existed for that team.
- My Downloads now cleans itself up automatically if a file gets deleted or moved outside the app — no more dead entries.
- If the marketplace fails to load for a second (bad connection, etc.), you now get a clear "Couldn't load — Retry" message instead of it just looking empty.

**🎨 UI fixes**
- Fixed the left sidebar (Teams/Save/Help) stretching to fill the whole window with a weird empty gap at the bottom.

**🔒 Security**
- Patched a path issue in the default song pack importer and a minor XSS gap in My Downloads. Nothing was actively exploited — found and fixed proactively.

**❓ Got questions about TeamBuilder / custom teams?**
Yes — a custom school you add via TeamBuilder works exactly like any real team. Bandroom doesn't recognize teams by looking at logos/colors on screen at all — it just needs you to pick Home/Away in the matchup screen, and it reads who has the ball from a simple on-screen position check. Your custom team also gets its own profile automatically, just like Georgia or Alabama would — no extra setup needed.

**📖 New: Help & Guide**
There's a full Tips & Tricks + step-by-step guide now — click the **Help & Guide** button in the sidebar (bottom-left). ~40 tips covering installing songs, sharing/loading profiles, editing logos, and more.
