🎺 **Bandroom Update — v1.0.1**

**💾 New: a real Save Profile button**
No more wondering if your setup actually saved. Hit the new **Save** button in the left rail — type a name (defaults to your current team) and it saves. You'll see a confirmation with the exact save time, and you can save under a different name any time to keep extra profiles around instead of always overwriting your team's main one.

**🏈 New: pick your matchup, both teams' songs ready to go**
Hit **Set Matchup** in the header and pick who's home and away for the game you're watching. Bandroom loads each team's own saved profile automatically — so if you've customized both sides over time, the right song fires for whichever team actually scores or forces a turnover, not just whoever you've got loaded in the sidebar.

**🎯 Fixed: touchdown/turnover sounds firing for the wrong team**
Previously, a touchdown always played *your* offense sound, and a forced turnover always played *your* defense sound — even when the other team did it. With a Matchup set, Bandroom now reads who actually has the ball off the scorebug and fires the correct side's sound.

**🧹 Cleaned up the left rail**
Removed two dead buttons that did nothing you couldn't already do elsewhere (Categories and the old Assign icon both just duplicated clicking a category chip). Left rail is now Teams / Save / Help.

**🐛 Fixed: a dialog button that looked broken**
The header's "Set Matchup" button was unclickable for a bit — a leftover window-drag handler was swallowing the click. Fixed.

---
**📌 Reminder on installing:**
Only run `BandroomSetup.exe` **once** — it puts one shortcut on your Desktop. After that, always use that same shortcut, and update *inside* the app when the ↑ button lights up. Don't keep old installer downloads around — delete `BandroomSetup.exe` from your Downloads right after you run it, so you're never tempted to double-click an old one later.

Full install guide: `INSTALL.md` in the repo.
