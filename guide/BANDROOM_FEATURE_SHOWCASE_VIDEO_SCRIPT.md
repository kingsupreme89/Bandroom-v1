### [3:30] 2.1 — LUFS Volume Normalization

**[On screen: Show three songs in Sound Bank — one quiet live recording, one loud album rip, one compressed YouTube download]**

**You say:**
"Here's the #1 complaint about custom audio in any app: all your songs are at different volumes. This one is whisper-quiet. This one will blow your speakers. This one is somewhere in between."

"Bandroom fixes this automatically. It's called LUFS normalization. LUFS measures how loud something FEELS to human ears — not just the electrical volume."

**[On screen: Show the normalization process — a song being analyzed, then a gain value applied]**

"Every time you import a song, Bandroom analyzes it using the same standard Netflix and Spotify use. It measures the overall loudness, then applies exactly the right amount of gain — volume boost or cut — so every single song plays at the same perceived volume."

"Marching band songs are normalized to -14 LUFS — punchy and present. PA announcer clips go to -18 LUFS — speech needs more headroom. Short stings and whistles hit at -12 LUFS — they need to cut through."

"The result: you never touch the volume knob. Every song, every trigger, every time — consistent."

**[On screen: Show the EQ settings panel. Play a marching band recording BEFORE and AFTER EQ.]**

### [8:00] 2.4 — Brickwall Limiter: Speaker Protection

**[On screen: Show a chaotic moment — touchdown + crowd roar + PA announcement all firing at once. Without limiter: clipping, distortion. With limiter: clean, controlled.]**

**You say:**
"In a real football game, multiple things happen at once. A touchdown triggers the fight song. The crowd roars. The PA announcer fires. All at the same time."

"Without protection, those sounds add up. They clip. They distort. They can even damage speakers."

**[On screen: Show the limiter settings — ceiling at -0.3 dBFS, 5ms look-ahead, 50ms release]**

"Bandroom's brickwall limiter puts a hard ceiling on volume. No matter how many sounds play at once, the combined output NEVER exceeds -0.3 dBFS — the broadcast-safe standard."

"It looks ahead 5 milliseconds. If the combined audio is about to clip, it instantly turns everything down just enough to stay safe. It releases after 50 milliseconds so it doesn't affect the next moment."

"This is speaker protection. This is ear protection. This is what separates pro audio from amateur audio."

## VIDEO PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Sound demonstrations are CRITICAL.** This video is about audio. Play the before/after for EQ and reverb. Don't just describe it — let viewers HEAR the difference.
- **Live demos over slides.** Show Bandroom running. Show the game. Show the chat. Real demonstrations are more convincing than any explanation.
- **Pacing:** 25 minutes is long. Keep sections moving. If a feature takes more than 90 seconds to explain, you're going too deep for this video.
- **"One week in" framing:** Mention this at the start and the end. Don't harp on it. The message is "it works, it's being improved daily, help me tune it."
- **Text overlays for technical terms.** When you say "LUFS" or "DSP" or "OCR," put the full term on screen briefly.
- **End screen:** Make it actionable. Download link + Discord + "subscribe for updates" — all three visible at once.
